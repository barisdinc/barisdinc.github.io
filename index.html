ASELSAN radios

ASELSAN 4000

Reverse engineering of the ASELSAN 4000 series VHF/UHF radio firmware (Mitsubishi M37732): full disassembly, a 7700 assembler, custom firmware examples, and a simulator that runs the original firmware in the browser.

ASELSAN 4000 serisi telsiz yazılımının çözümlenmesi ve tarayıcıda çalışan simülatör.

ASELSAN 4822

New-generation modifications for the ASELSAN 4822 / 4826: replacement controller boards (Arduino Nano, ESP32, Raspberry Pi Pico) with many new features, plus service documentation.

ASELSAN 4822 için yeni kontrol kartları ve özellikler.

DATV · digital amateur television

ESP32-DATV-SA

Stand-alone DVB-S2 transmitter and beacon on an ESP32-C3, using the chip's own 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi radio. No PC needed. Fork of SP8ESA/ESP32-DATV.

PC gerektirmeyen ESP32-C3 DVB-S2 verici.

ESP32-DATV-RX

DVB-S2 receiver for ESP32-DATV-SA: an ESP32-S3 captures the Wi-Fi receiver's raw I/Q and streams it over USB; the PC demodulates.

ESP32-S3 ile DVB-S2 alıcı.

yATOLYE DATV Repeater

2.4 GHz to 5.8 GHz DATV repeater on the ADALM-PLUTO SDR. The RX-to-TX relay runs in the FPGA, with an optional DVB-S2 beacon.

ADALM-PLUTO üzerinde DATV röle.

QO-100 · satellite listening

QO-100 WebSDR Pocket Radio

ESP8266 internet radio that listens to 10489.777 MHz USB on the BATC or IS0GRB WebSDR. Captive-portal WiFi setup, a web UI for fine tuning, squelch and volume, and sigma-delta audio from one pin.

WebSDR üzerinden QO-100 dinleyen ESP8266 cep radyosu.
